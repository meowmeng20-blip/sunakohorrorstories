THE PHANTOM TRUCK OF NORTH CAROLINA

Sender: Dark Lord • Unexplained Mystery

We have never figured this out. And now, the three living witnesses have to be good and drunk to discuss the whole thing.

I was 7, my brother 10, my mom in her early 40s, and my grandmother in her 60s. We were all cogent. No one was too young or too senile not to recall this nonsense. Yet, still no bloody answer.

Grandma lived on an isolated country road in North Carolina that was named after her family, since they were the only ones who lived on the land for about 1,000 acres. Her house had been empty for several weeks while she visited us in Florida, but we were all back spending the weekend before trekking back to the Sunshine State. The house is deep in the country—literally over train tracks, past a salvage yard, and her nearest neighbor (a cousin) wasn’t within screaming distance.

It was early morning, just before daybreak. We were eating cereal when we heard someone pull up outside. Curious, we all ran to the big picture window facing the front yard.

The Mystery Vehicle:

"There was a strange truck sitting there. No one seemed to be behind the wheel, though the engine was idling. The truck was old-timey—like something from the 1930s. Rusted, but probably once painted blue."

Bewildered, Mom asked Grandma if she knew who that was. Grandma had no clue. She ran to get the phone to call her cousin to see if it was a hired hand who had the wrong farm. But just as she asked him to come down, the phone line went completely dead.

All at once, there was a loud, insistent banging on the front door. We all screamed.

My grandma huddled us into the living room, away from the windows. While we trembled on the couch, she grabbed a serrated bread knife from the kitchen, cautiously approached the door, and peeked out a side window. She turned back looking utterly confused and shook her head—no one was there.

Then EVERY door in the house started banging relentlessly.

Relentless Attack:

"Rhythmic and terrifying, like all the doors were about to splinter and crack. Two doors in the basement beneath us vibrated at our feet. Three ground-floor doors were shaking and trembling on their hinges."

Mom ran to the window and cried, "Oh thank Christ—Cousin is here!" We peeked out and saw Cousin walking past the truck holding a shotgun. He looked puzzled, checked the rear, glanced into the cab window, and froze. He went pale, ran a hand down his face, and sprinted toward the house.

Grandma flung open the kitchen door. "Everyone get behind the couch! Get DOWN!" he shouted. He ran past us as we bolted for cover.

The banging started AGAIN—all doors and rattling windows. It felt like a tornado or the end of the world. Cousin flung open the front door and fired a deafening shotgun shot into the yard: BANG!

As he fired, the truck roared to life, sounding like a train engine. The banging stopped. Cousin advanced onto the lawn, gun leveled. The truck pealed out backward, cutting across the yard at breakneck speed. Cousin fired a second shot, blowing out the rear window with a sound like a thousand plates smashing, but the truck roared down the road without even a hiccup. No tags, no vanity plate.

The Chilling Truth:

There was NO ONE behind the wheel of that truck. We all had a clear view. Not a single driver in the cab.

Cousin had to drive to his house to call the police because our phone line was cut. When police investigated, there was not a single boot print in the damp yard except for Cousin's.

Cousin later reported that when he looked into the cab, it looked like something from a horror movie—packed with weird restraints (handcuffs, c-clamps, nylon straps) and floorboards soaked in what smelled like blood.

Grandma and Cousin have since passed away, holding firm to their unchanging accounts until their last days. To this day, the three remaining witnesses still don't know how every door and window was banging at once, or how anyone could move around the house without leaving a single footprint in the damp earth.

Some roads hold secrets that defy reason, leaving behind only unanswered questions and phantom engine roars in the night.
