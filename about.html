About Sunako Horror Stories

Welcome to Sunako Horror Stories, a dedicated digital repository for dark folklore, true terrifying encounters, Philippine urban legends, and eerie paranormal experiences in Tagalog and English.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build a immersive, clean, and performant reading platform for horror enthusiasts worldwide. We strive to preserve haunting local storytelling while discovering chilling new voices in the horror community.

What We Offer

  • True Personal Encounters: Eerie stories sent by real people.
  • Philippine Urban Legends: Classic and modern tales of folklore and supernatural creatures.
  • Bilingual Reads: Stories carefully curated in both Tagalog and English.
