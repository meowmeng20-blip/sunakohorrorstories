About Sunako Horror Stories

Welcome to Sunako Horror Stories, a dedicated digital repository for dark folklore, true paranormal experiences, urban legends, and eerie tales from the Philippines and beyond.

Our Mission

Our goal is to preserve and share terrifying real-life encounters, traditional beliefs (such as barang, kulam, and albularyo healing), creepy dorm encounters, and haunting legends with horror enthusiasts globally.

Community & Submissions

We actively accept story contributions from readers and writers. Every story submitted is carefully published to provide an immersive reading experience with modern, dark-themed responsive design.

Have a horror story of your own? Send it over to sunakohorrorstories@gmail.com and be featured on our website!
