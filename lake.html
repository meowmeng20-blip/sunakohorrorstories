THE LAKE ACCIDENT

Sender: Anonymous • Supernatural Mystery

Three college students named Devin, Bethany, and Seth were driving along a lonely mountain road when, all of a sudden, they hit a rock. The car swerved off the edge of a cliff and plunged into a lake.

When they all emerged from the water, they breathed a sigh of relief, happy to still be alive.

However, when they looked around, they were horrified to see their own bodies, still trapped inside the sinking car.

Stunned, the three friends swam to the shore and tried to figure out what had happened.

“I know what I saw,” Bethany said. “Those were our bodies in the car.”

“When it hit the water, I felt my neck snap,” Devin said. “The next thing I remember, we were in the water.”

“I don’t remember getting out of the car either,” said Seth.

“We must have died in the wreck,” Devin said quietly. “You guys… I think we’re ghosts.”

Just then, a truck came speeding down the road and almost hit them.

“Get out of the road, you morons!” the driver shouted.

“He saw us!” Seth said. “That guy saw us, so we can’t be dead!”

“We are dead,” Devin insisted. “We saw our bodies.”

“But how could we die and still be alive?” Seth asked.

The Chilling Paradox:

“Nobody witnessed the accident,” Bethany said. “If a tree falls in the forest and there’s no one there to hear it, does it make a sound? Maybe the reason we’re still alive is that we’re the only ones who know that we’re dead…”

The three of them went back to college and tried to act like nothing happened.

“Cheer up,” Seth laughed. “This is the first day of the rest of our lives.”

Devin was still haunted by the fact that he had cheated death. He felt like it was a sin. “When I think of myself down in that car,” he said, “I feel like I belong there.”

Devin was very religious and needed advice, so he went to see a priest for confession.

“Bless me father for I have sinned,” he said. “I was in a car crash. Two of my friends were killed. No one saw it happen. No one knows.”

“You haven’t told the police?” the priest asked, “or your friends’ families?”

“No,” Devin replied.

“Let me help,” said the priest. “I can break the news to your friends’ loved ones. What are their names?”

Devin took a deep breath. “Seth Moran,” he whispered.

All of a sudden, Seth disappeared into thin air.

“Bethany Daniels,” he whispered.

Bethany suddenly disappeared as well.

“There was one more,” Devin whispered. “Me. I died in the crash.”

The priest didn’t believe a word of it. “Devin, this is absurd,” he said. “You’re talking nonsense.”

Bethany and Seth reappeared, just as suddenly as they had vanished, coughing, choking, and throwing up lake water.

Back in their campus dorm, Bethany and Seth were desperate to stay alive. They were worried that Devin was going to ruin everything.

“If he can’t keep his mouth shut, we’re all going to die,” said Seth.

“The car is only 10 feet down,” said Bethany. “Sooner or later, someone is going to find the car… and us.”

“We’ve got to get our bodies out of there,” said Seth. “We can bury them in the woods.”

“Deep,” Bethany agreed. “As deep as we can.”

Devin still couldn’t come to terms with what happened. He thought his salvation depended on his body being found, so he went back to the lake. When Bethany and Seth realized what he was up to, they raced back to the lake to stop him.

Devin dived into the water and swam down to the car, which was perched precariously on a narrow outcropping underwater.

Peering through the window, Devin could see his own dead body… Seth’s body… and Bethany’s body.

He couldn’t open the car door, so he pulled back the canopy. He tried to pull his own body out, but it was wedged tightly between the steering wheel and the seat. No matter how hard he tried, he couldn’t budge it.

However, as he pulled and tugged, he accidentally freed the bodies of Seth and Bethany. They floated up toward the surface.

Devin was horrified when the car tipped over and plunged deep down into the abyss, taking his body with it.

Bethany and Seth arrived at the shore just in time to see their own corpses floating on the water's surface. A passing fisherman spotted the bodies and rowed his boat closer to take a look.

The Eternal Curse:

Devin swam back up to the surface. As he watched in horror, the moment the fisherman confirmed the bodies, Bethany and Seth vanished forever.

“Oh God no!” he screamed. “It was supposed to be me, not them! It was supposed to be me!”

Devin was doomed to wander the Earth for all eternity. His body would never be found. He would never be able to die. He would never be at peace.

Some secrets are better left submerged in the depths, where no eyes can ever bear witness.
